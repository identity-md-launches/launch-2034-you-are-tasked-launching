THE FINE PRINT, IN PLAIN ENGLISH

KNOW THE
MECHANICS.

Is this the first ERC20T token?+

No first-ever claim is made. The factory’s first clone already existed when this project was prepared. The app scans all creation events and reports the exact ordinal only for a confirmed launch.

What does “locked liquidity” mean?+

The factory’s token implementation mints the initial Uniswap v4 position NFT to its fixed burn address. The launch application checks that recipient and verifies nonzero liquidity in the correct pool. The owner cannot withdraw that position through this application. A lock does not guarantee a token price.

Where do trading taxes go?+

Both the resting fee recipient and fee decay recipient are the designated owner at launch. The hook converts collected fees to ETH. Its protocol recipient receives a 15 bps share taken from the token tax: at the 1% resting rate, 15% of converted fees go to the protocol and 85% go to the owner. The conversion pool’s costs and price impact affect ETH payouts. The schedule is 99% initially, dropping by 100 bps every 60 seconds to 1%. The token retains the infrastructure’s owner permissions, including recipient and distributor controls. Resting-tax changes are blocked during the launch decay, then limited to 30–500 bps. Recheck live settings before trading.

Why is the token address still pending?+

The application must first be deployed through the project handoff. Its owner then confirms the factory launch in their wallet. This site reads the resulting token address and verifies the transaction receipt; it never invents an address.

Can I use any connected wallet?+

Any wallet can inspect chain data. Only the designated owner can launch. The app verifies Ethereum mainnet, the application bytecode and the wallet account before requesting a transaction.
